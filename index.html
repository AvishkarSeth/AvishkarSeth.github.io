Drones that swap batteries without landing.

I'm Dr Avishkar Seth, Senior Research Officer in robotics and drone systems at Macquarie University's Advanced Drone Systems Research Centre. I work on aerial autonomy, sensing systems and hardware-software integration, and I lead Dronergy, a startup commercialising mid-air battery swapping.
