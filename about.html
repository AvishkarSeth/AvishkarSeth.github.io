Dr Avishkar Seth

Senior Research Officer, Robotics and Drone Systems

Advanced Drone Systems Research Centre, founding member. Macquarie research profile

I'm a Senior Research Officer (robotics and drone systems) at Macquarie University, specialising in aerial autonomy, avionics and physical sensing systems. I turn complex theory into field-ready hardware: taking systems off the bench, testing under real constraints, and bridging research and commercial impact.

I also lead Dronergy, a startup commercialising mid-air battery swapping for drones.

Driven by curiosity and a bias for physical reality, I'm always open to collaborations in robotics, hard tech and applied engineering.

Away from the lab: music, photography, favourite shows, travel, science and tech, nature and the universe, gaming.
