Teaching

From 2020 to December 2025 I taught embedded systems, IoT and mobile robotics at Macquarie University, with one aim: make autonomous systems approachable, and help students see the creative thinking behind complex theory. I still mentor student teams in the MQ Robotics Club, Drone Society, MARS Rover and the Advanced Drone Systems Research Centre.
